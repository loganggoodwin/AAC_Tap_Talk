Privacy Policy for AAC Tap Talk

Effective Date: May 9, 2026

AAC Tap Talk is developed by Frontline aka Besttech / Logan Goodwin. This app is designed to provide simple assistive communication buttons, including Yes, No, and Bathroom, with speech output.

Information We Collect

AAC Tap Talk does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require users to create an account and does not ask for a name, email address, location, photos, contacts, or any other personal data.

Children's Privacy

AAC Tap Talk may be used by children, including children under 13, with parent, guardian, caregiver, or educator support. The app does not collect personal information from children.

Device Data

AAC Tap Talk does not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, location data, usage analytics, crash analytics, or tracking data.

Internet and Third-Party Services

AAC Tap Talk does not require an internet connection and does not use advertising, analytics, social media integrations, or third-party tracking services.

Speech Output

AAC Tap Talk uses the Android device's built-in Text-to-Speech feature to speak selected words or phrases aloud. Speech is generated on the user's device through Android system functionality.

Data Sharing

Since AAC Tap Talk does not collect user data, no user data is shared with anyone.

Data Retention and Deletion

AAC Tap Talk does not store user data. Because no user data is collected or stored, there is no user data to delete.

Security

AAC Tap Talk is designed to avoid collecting personal or sensitive data. The app does not transmit user data over the internet.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app changes in the future. Any updates will be posted on this page.
