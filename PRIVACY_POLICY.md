# Privacy Policy for AAC Tap Talk

Effective date: May 9, 2026

AAC Tap Talk is a simple communication support app that lets users tap picture buttons for Yes, No, and Bathroom. The app displays the selected choice and speaks it aloud using Android's built-in text-to-speech system.

## Information We Collect

AAC Tap Talk does not collect personal information.

The app does not require an account, does not ask for a name, does not collect contact information, does not collect location data, and does not collect health information.

## Data Sharing

AAC Tap Talk does not share user data with third parties.

## Internet Access

AAC Tap Talk does not request internet permission and does not transmit information over the internet.

## Advertising and Analytics

AAC Tap Talk does not contain ads and does not use analytics services.

## Children’s Privacy

AAC Tap Talk is designed as a simple communication support tool and may be used by children with help from a parent, guardian, teacher, caregiver, or therapist. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Text-to-Speech

AAC Tap Talk uses the Android device's built-in text-to-speech feature to speak selected choices aloud. Speech output is handled by the device's installed text-to-speech service.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app changes. Any future changes should be reflected on the public privacy policy page used for the Google Play listing.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the app developer.
